### Reuse Ingredients Across Meals
<!--
Minimize "stops" at the store by planning meals that share ingredients.
- If buying cilantro for tacos Tuesday, use it in Thai curry Thursday
- Rotisserie chicken can become: dinner → chicken salad → soup
- Buy one type of protein in bulk, use different preparations
-->

### Minimize Waste
<!--
- Plan meals that use whole ingredients (use broccoli stems too)
- Leafy greens early in the week, hardier vegetables later
- Freeze what won't be used in time
-->

### Keep It Interesting
<!--
- Same ingredient, different cuisines (chicken: Italian, Mexican, Asian)
- Vary cooking methods (grilled, roasted, stir-fried)
- One "new" recipe per week, rest are familiar favorites
